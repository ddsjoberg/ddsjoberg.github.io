About

I lead the Data Science and Clinical Analytics groups at Kardigan, where I focus on building high-performing teams and modernizing how clinical data are reviewed, analyzed, and reported. My work centers on streamlining end-to-end processes, adopting modern analytical and software toolkits, and creating scalable, reproducible approaches that help teams move from data to decisions more efficiently.

Previously, I was a Senior Principal Innovation Data Scientist and Real-Time Analytics Group Lead at Genentech, a Lead Data Science Manager at the Prostate Cancer Clinical Trials Consortium, and an academic biostatistician at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center for 13 years.

I remain passionate about open-source software and the R community. I have developed numerous R packages available through CRAN, R-Universe, and GitHub, and I am a co-organizer of the rainbowR organization and the R Medicine Conference. I am also a recipient of the Brian Bole Award for Excellence from Open Source in Pharma and the Innovation in Programming Award from the American Statistical Association.

In my free time, I enjoy baking, going to the beach, and running.
