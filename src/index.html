About

I lead the Data Science and Clinical Analytics groups at Kardigan. Previously, I was a Senior Principal Innovation Data Scientist and Real-Time Analytics Group Lead at Genentech, a Lead Data Science Manager at the Prostate Cancer Clinical Trials Consortium, and an academic biostatistician at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center for 13 years.

I enjoy R package development and have created many packages on CRAN, R-Universe, and GitHub. I am a co-organizer of rainbowR and of the R Medicine Conference, and a recipient of the Brian Bole Award for Excellence from Open Source in Pharma and the Innovation in Programming Award from the American Statistical Association.

In my free time, I enjoy baking, going to the beach, and running.
